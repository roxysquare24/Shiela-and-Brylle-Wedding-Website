The Beginning

What started as simple visits to her home slowly became the beginning of something beautiful.

The First Date

No fancy restaurants. No expensive plans. Just kwek-kwek, mango shake, and time spent together at The Bay. Some of the best memories came from the simplest moments.

Simple Days

He often visited her home, bringing tamban to grill and bananas to share. Neither of them needed grand gestures. They simply enjoyed spending time together and finding happiness in life's simplest moments.

First Adventure

Sometimes the best trips are the ones that were never planned. A simple "Tara, laag ta" became one of the most unforgettable adventures they shared together.

Long Distance

Much of their story was written through distance. Through calls, messages, patience, and trust, they continued choosing each other every single day.

Our Little Family

Along the way, life blessed them with their greatest adventure yet. Their love grew into a beautiful family, making every sacrifice and every journey worthwhile.

The Next Chapter

After every conversation, every journey, every sacrifice, and every mile apart, they are now preparing to begin forever together.
